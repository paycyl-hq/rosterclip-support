Privacy Policy

RosterClip prioritizes your privacy. Your schedule data and calendar events are stored locally on your device. However, to provide smart shift scheduling, the images or text you upload are securely sent to our AI service provider strictly for parsing and data extraction.

This data is processed temporarily, is not stored on external servers, and is not used to train AI models. We do not collect, store, or share your personal calendar data with any other third parties.

Third-Party Services

While we do not collect personal data on our servers, our app uses third-party services that may collect information to process in-app purchases or serve advertisements:

  • RevenueCat: We use RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions. They may collect anonymous user identifiers and purchase history to validate your premium status.
  • Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to serve advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as Advertising ID), usage data, and diagnostic data (such as crash logs and performance metrics) to provide and improve their advertising services.
